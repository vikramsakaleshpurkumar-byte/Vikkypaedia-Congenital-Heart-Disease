# 1. Introduction: Formulation of the problem

The same congenital lesion can behave differently in a fetus, a newborn and an adult. Begin with anatomy and physiology, then predict the findings.

Audience: Pediatric residents, cardiology trainees and clinicians studying congenital heart disease from infancy to adulthood
Prerequisites: Core cardiovascular anatomy and physiology; chapters1–2 introduce the diagnostic approach and functional murmurs.
