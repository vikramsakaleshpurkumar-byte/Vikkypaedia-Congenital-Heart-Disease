<details><summary>Which two discordances produce the physiologically corrected circulation?</summary>

Atrioventricular and ventriculoarterial discordance. Two discordant junctions place systemic venous blood in the pulmonary circuit and pulmonary venous blood in the systemic circuit, so the circulations are in series.

</details>

<details><summary>Which ventricular morphology supports the systemic circulation in the typical double-discordant arrangement?</summary>

The systemic morphologic right ventricle must sustain a workload for which its usual geometry and blood supply are not designed. The systemic right ventricle retains right-ventricular geometry and a morphologically right coronary supply despite systemic loading.

PDF p. 71
</details>

<details><summary>Which morphologic valve can have an Ebstein-like malformation in the left atrioventricular position?</summary>

Morphologic tricuspid valve. The inverted left atrioventricular valve is morphologically tricuspid; Ebstein-like abnormalities and age-related regurgitation are important.

PDF p. 72
</details>

<details><summary>What change in the long penetrating atrioventricular bundle can cause progressive block?</summary>

Replacement of conduction fibers by fibrous tissue in the long penetrating bundle. Fibrous replacement in the long penetrating bundle contributes to age-related atrioventricular block; defects may occur at several conduction levels.

PDF p. 77
</details>

<details><summary>What precordial Q-wave pattern follows reversed septal depolarization?</summary>

Q waves in right precordial leads with absent left precordial Q waves. Inverted bundle branches activate the septum from right to left, reversing the usual precordial septal Q-wave pattern.

PDF p. 78
</details>

<details><summary>Which echocardiographic features identify a morphologic right ventricle rather than relying on its side of the chest?</summary>

Coarse trabeculation, a moderator band, relatively distal atrioventricular valve insertion and tricuspid morphology help identify the morphologic right ventricle.

PDF p. 81
</details>
