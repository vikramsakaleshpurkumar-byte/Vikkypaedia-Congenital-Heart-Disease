<details><summary>Why can double discordance produce a circulation in series?</summary>

Two discordant connections restore the appropriate destinations of blood. Atrioventricular and ventriculoarterial discordance can together produce physiologic correction of blood-flow destinations.

Right isomerism. Right isomerism describes bilateral morphologically right characteristics in structures normally different on the two sides.
