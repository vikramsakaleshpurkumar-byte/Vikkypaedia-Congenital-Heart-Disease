<details><summary>How can left ventricular failure alter an aortic stenosis murmur?</summary>

Reduced murmur intensity does not establish reduced obstruction. Aortic stenosis murmur intensity can decrease in left ventricular failure; loudness is not a stand-alone measure of obstruction.

Image the open valve for two unequal doming leaflets and an elliptical orifice. Open-valve imaging reveals the bicuspid anatomy that can be obscured by a false raphe during closure.

Rapid rise, wide pulse pressure and brisk collapse. Chronic severe regurgitation produces a rapid upstroke, wide pulse pressure, sometimes a bisferiens peak, and brisk collapse.
