Both arterial valves have most of their circumference supported within a morphologic right ventricle in a heart with two ventricles and concordant atrioventricular connections. Which designation fits?

A. Complete transposition defined only by discordant arterial connections
B. Double outlet right ventricle
C. Double inlet left ventricle
D. An isolated ventricular communication with normal arterial origins

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Double outlet right ventricle

The chapter uses ventricular support of most of both arterial valves as its pragmatic definition while acknowledging classification debates.

## Authored MCQ

Both great arteries arise predominantly from the right ventricle, and the ventricular communication is directly beneath the pulmonary outlet. Which streaming pattern is favored?

A. Left ventricular blood can leave only through the mitral valve
B. All left ventricular blood must preferentially enter the aorta
C. Left ventricular blood preferentially enters the pulmonary trunk
D. The location of the communication cannot affect streaming

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Left ventricular blood preferentially enters the pulmonary trunk

A subpulmonary communication directs left ventricular output toward the pulmonary outlet, creating a transposition-like physiology.

## Authored MCQ

An infant has a nonrestrictive subaortic ventricular communication, both arteries mainly supported by the right ventricle, and low pulmonary resistance without pulmonary stenosis. Which clinical pattern is expected?

A. Obligatory profound cyanosis from pulmonary atresia
B. A large ventricular left-to-right shunt with increased pulmonary flow
C. A normal circulation without recirculation
D. An isolated atrial shunt with no ventricular loading

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A large ventricular left-to-right shunt with increased pulmonary flow

Low pulmonary resistance permits increased pulmonary flow and left ventricular volume overload, resembling a large isolated ventricular defect.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A child with subaortic commitment develops severe pulmonary outflow obstruction. Which direction of change is expected?

A. Less pulmonary flow and more blood directed to the aorta
B. More pulmonary flow regardless of obstruction severity
C. Mandatory elimination of every ventricular pressure gradient
D. Lower systemic oxygenation only if the aortic valve closes

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Less pulmonary flow and more blood directed to the aorta

Obstruction diverts both ventricular streams away from the pulmonary outlet and toward the aorta, producing a tetralogy-like pattern.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

A subaortic ventricular communication narrows in a heart whose arterial outlets are both mainly right ventricular. What consequence should be assessed?

A. A new atrial septal defect as the required consequence
B. Assured benefit equivalent to closure of an uncomplicated isolated small defect
C. Loss of pulmonary venous return as the direct anatomic effect
D. Obstruction to left ventricular egress and subaortic flow

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Obstruction to left ventricular egress and subaortic flow

The communication is an essential left ventricular exit; restriction can act as subaortic stenosis rather than a uniformly favorable reduction in shunt.

</details>
