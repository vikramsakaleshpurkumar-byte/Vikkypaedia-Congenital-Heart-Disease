## Authored MCQ

The septal and posterior tricuspid leaflet attachments lie abnormally far toward the apex. Which region becomes functionally integrated with the right atrium?

A. Only the right atrial appendage proximal to the annulus
B. The inlet right ventricle between the anatomic annulus and displaced functional valve
C. The entire left ventricle
D. The distal right ventricular outlet beyond the functional valve

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The inlet right ventricle between the anatomic annulus and displaced functional valve

Apical displacement atrializes the proximal inlet right ventricle while the trabecular and outlet portions form the functional right ventricle.

## Authored MCQ

An adult has substantial tricuspid regurgitation but lacks striking systolic jugular pulsation. Which lesion-specific mechanism explains this?

A. Absence of a V wave proves the valve is competent
B. A large compliant right atrium and toneless atrialized ventricle damp low-pressure regurgitant flow
C. The jugular pulse reflects only the left atrium
D. Every regurgitant jet has the same pressure and turbulence

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A large compliant right atrium and toneless atrialized ventricle damp low-pressure regurgitant flow

Commodious right-sided chambers and low-pressure hypokinetic regurgitation can damp the expected jugular signs.

A neonatal right-to-left atrial shunt can diminish as pulmonary resistance falls and reappear when abnormal right ventricular filling pressure rises

The source describes this uncommon course through changing right-sided filling conditions and interatrial shunting.

## Authored MCQ

An adolescent has paroxysmal rapid heart action; the resting ECG shows a short PR interval and early delta-wave inscription. Which interpretation is supported?

A. A normal PR interval prolonged by intra-atrial delay
B. An isolated short PR interval with no additional evidence
C. First-degree atrioventricular block
D. Preexcitation

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Preexcitation

Early delta-wave inscription supports preexcitation; a short PR interval alone can occasionally occur without a delta wave or tachycardia history.
