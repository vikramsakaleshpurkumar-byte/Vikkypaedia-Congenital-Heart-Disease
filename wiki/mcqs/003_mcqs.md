## Authored MCQ

An otherwise normally arranged heart shifts within the chest because of an abnormal diaphragm. Which term best describes this positional change?

A. Bilateral right atrial isomerism by definition
B. Secondary cardiac displacement
C. Ventriculoarterial discordance by definition
D. Visceroatrial situs inversus by definition

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Secondary cardiac displacement

Cardiac displacement is a secondary shift in intrathoracic position, such as one produced by diaphragmatic eventration.

An image shows an unusual cardiac position. Which next approach most reliably describes the circulation?

A. Infer all intracardiac connections from the gastric bubble
B. Identify the atria, valves, ventricles and arterial connections systematically
C. Assign chamber identities solely from the side of the chest
D. Assume every right-sided heart has identical physiology

The source describes systematic attention to morphology, atrioventricular connections, ventriculoarterial connections and great-artery relationships.

## Authored MCQ

A radiograph shows a right thoracic heart, a right-sided stomach and a left-sided liver. Which arrangement best fits?

A. Situs inversus with levocardia
B. An isolated rightward shift that establishes normal visceral positions
C. Situs inversus with dextrocardia
D. Situs solitus with a left thoracic heart

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Situs inversus with dextrocardia

The described heart and abdominal viscera are in the mirror-image positions of the usual arrangement.

## Authored MCQ

Systemic venous blood passes from a morphologic right atrium through a morphologic left ventricle to the pulmonary artery, while pulmonary venous blood reaches the aorta through a morphologic right ventricle. Which explanation fits?

A. Two discordant connections restore the appropriate destinations of blood
B. The atrioventricular connections must therefore be normal
C. Both circuits must run in parallel solely because the aorta arises from a morphologic right ventricle
D. Both arterial trunks must arise from a single ventricle

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Two discordant connections restore the appropriate destinations of blood

Atrioventricular and ventriculoarterial discordance can together produce physiologic correction of blood-flow destinations.

</details>

## Authored MCQ

Imaging identifies bilateral morphologically right atrial appendages and bilateral trilobed lungs. Which term describes this pattern?

A. Normal right–left asymmetry
B. Simple cardiac displacement
C. Left isomerism
D. Right isomerism

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Right isomerism

Right isomerism describes bilateral morphologically right characteristics in structures normally different on the two sides.
